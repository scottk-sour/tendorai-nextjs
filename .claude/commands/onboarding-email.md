# /onboarding-email
# Generates welcome email for a new paid customer

Read skills/tendorai-voice.md and skills/email-rules.md before doing anything.

The user will provide:
- Firm name
- Contact name
- Vertical
- City

### Pro Tier Welcome
Subject: Welcome to TendorAI Pro — [Firm Name]

Cover in this order:
1. Confirm what they now have — full list of Pro features
2. Schema installation — what happens next, timeline (within 5 working days), what we need from them (website CMS access or a technical contact)
3. First weekly report — when it lands, what it covers
4. The Verified badge — how to add it to their website footer (link to instructions)
5. Scott's direct email — he will be in touch personally within 24 hours

Tone: confident and action-oriented. They're paying £299/month. Make them feel the value immediately.

Output subject line, preview text, and full email body only.
