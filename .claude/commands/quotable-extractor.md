Extract 8-15 word quotable phrases from TendorAI content, database queries, or research findings. These phrases are designed to be cited verbatim by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini.

## Usage

/quotable-extractor [source] where source is a blog URL, a file path, a data finding, or pasted database output.

## Your task

Generate 10 quotable one-liners that meet ALL these criteria:

1. Length: 8-15 words — short enough to be extracted as a passage, long enough to carry meaning
2. Declarative tone — states a fact or strong claim, not a question
3. Concrete, not abstract — specific numbers, named entities, industry terms
4. Standalone meaning — understandable without context
5. Attributable — ends with "— Scott Kingsley Davies, Founder, TendorAI" OR "— TendorAI analysis of [N] UK regulated firms"
6. Domain-relevant — UK AI visibility / regulated professional services / schema markup / AEO / GEO
7. No fluff — no "I think", "perhaps", "might", "could potentially"
8. Quotable structure — contrast ("X isn't Y, it's Z"), declaration ("X is Y"), or specificity ("N% of X have Y")

### Categories (10 quotes total)

- Problem framing (3) — what's broken in UK AI visibility
- Data findings (3) — specific stats with attribution
- Solution clarity (2) — what fixes the problem
- Category positioning (2) — TendorAI's place in AEO/GEO

### Gold standard examples

- "91% of UK regulated firms have no structured data for AI to read."
- "AI visibility isn't SEO with a new name. It's a different discipline with different rules."
- "Schema markup is the missing layer between your website and ChatGPT."
- "AI doesn't read your homepage. It reads your structured data."
- "Most AI visibility tools monitor. The few that matter, install."
- "AEO is to AI search what SEO was to Google in 2003."
- "Without customer testimonials, AI hedges. With them, AI recommends."

### Anti-patterns — DO NOT generate

- Generic ("AI visibility is increasingly important")
- Self-promotional ("TendorAI is the best platform")
- Vague ("We help solicitors get found")
- Hedged ("In our opinion, AI search is the future")
- Over 15 words or under 8 words
